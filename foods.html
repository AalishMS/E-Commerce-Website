Food & Where to Eat in Patan

Popular Newari Foods

Yomari

Yomari is a traditional Newari sweet made from rice flour and filled with molasses and sesame seeds.

Choila

Choila is spicy grilled buffalo meat mixed with local spices. It is one of the most popular Newari dishes.

Bara

Bara is a soft lentil pancake that is usually served with egg or meat.

Chatamari

Chatamari is known as the Newari pizza because of its unique toppings.

Recommended Restaurants

Honacha Restaurant

Famous for authentic Newari food near Patan Durbar Square.

Cafe du Temple

A popular café with a beautiful view of Patan Durbar Square.

Himalayan Java

A great place for coffee, snacks, and desserts in Jawalakhel.

A peaceful café serving homemade food and coffee.
