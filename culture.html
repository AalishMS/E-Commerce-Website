Culture, Events & Visitor Tips

Craft Heritage

Patan is famous for metalwork and woodcarving. Skilled artists make beautiful statues, doors, and windows.

Festivals

Rato Machhindranath Jatra is the biggest festival in Patan. A large chariot is pulled through the city by many people.

Visitor Tips

  • Best Time: October–November and March–April
  • How to Get There: Taxi, bus, or scooter from Kathmandu.
  • Opening Hours: Open daily. Museums are open during the day.
  • Dress Code: Wear modest clothes and respect temple rules.
