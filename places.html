Places to Visit in Patan

Step into the ancient Newari city of Patan. Its a living museum of carved temples, palace squares, and courtyard shrines nestled in the Kathmandu Valley.

Plan Your Visit

Iconic Attractions

Patan Durbar Square

The heart of the old royal city, lined with the palace complex, ornate temples, and courtyard squares dating back to the Malla dynasty.

Location: Mangal Bazar, Patan

Learn More

Krishna Mandir

A masterpiece of stone carving, this three-tiered Shikhara-style temple was built by King Siddhi Narsingh Malla in the 17th century.

Location: Patan Durbar Square

Learn More

Golden Temple

A gilded Buddhist monastery hidden in a courtyard of the old town, shining with gold-plated roofs and intricate statues of the Buddha.

Location: Kwa Bahal, Patan

Learn More

Patan Museum

Housed in a restored wing of the royal palace, this museum displays some of the finest bronze and wooden art in South Asia.

Location: Patan Durbar Square

Learn More

Mahabouddha Temple

A terracotta temple crowned by thousands of small Buddha images, earning it the nickname the "Temple of a Thousand Buddhas".

Location: Oku Bahal, Patan

Learn More

Kumbeshwar Temple

A five-tiered pagoda temple raised on a stone plinth, dedicated to Shiva and surrounded by ancient waterspouts and shrine courtyards.

Location: Kumbeshwar Tole, Patan

Best Time to Visit

  • Rato Machindranath Jatra (April–May): Patan's biggest festival, with a massive chariot pulled through the streets over several weeks.
  • Krishna Janmashtami (Aug–Sep): Krishna Mandir in Durbar Square becomes the center of celebration, lit with oil lamps and packed with devotees.
  • Mataya Festival (Aug, after Gai Jatra): a Buddhist procession where locals walk barefoot visiting hundreds of shrines and monasteries around Patan.
  • Samyak (every 5 years): a rare, large-scale gathering of Buddha statues from monasteries across the valley, displayed in Patan Durbar Square.
  • Best general season: October–March, when the dry and cooler weather makes sightseeing more comfortable outside festival dates.
